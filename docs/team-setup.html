For the production team

Set up Video Factory on your computer

About 40 minutes, mostly waiting for downloads. No coding needed: you copy one line, paste it, and sign in a couple of times. At the end, you can make videos by talking to Claude, using every style, kit and tool the team has.

Before you start

  • A Windows 10/11 PC or a Mac, with at least 10 GB free disk space and good internet.
  • A Claude account on a paid plan (Pro or Max), and the Claude app installed.
  • That's it. No GitHub account and no passwords to remember.
1

Get your personal line from the owner

Ask the project owner for access. You'll get a message with two lines made just for you, one for Windows and one for Mac. They contain your own access key (the part starting with vfk_).

Keep your line private. Anyone with it can use your access. Don't post it in a group chat or share your screen while it's visible.

However long the owner takes to reply

2

Paste your line to install everything

  1. Click Start, type PowerShell, and open Windows PowerShell.
  2. Copy your Windows line from the owner's message, right-click inside the blue window to paste it, and press Enter. It looks like this:
irm "https://video-factory.lakshmansai121.workers.dev/install.ps1?key=vfk_…" | iex
  1. Press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, and press Enter.
  2. Copy your Mac line from the owner's message, paste it into Terminal with Cmd + V, and press Enter. It looks like this:
curl -fsSL "https://video-factory.lakshmansai121.workers.dev/install?key=vfk_…" | bash

While it runs, it will ask you for two things

  1. Your computer password, to install tools. On Windows this may be a few "Do you want to allow changes?" pop-ups: click Yes. On a Mac, type your password (nothing shows while you type, that's normal) and press Enter. If a Mac asks to install "command line developer tools", click Install.
  2. Claude sign-in. A browser page opens: sign in with your Claude account. If the window then asks for a code, copy it from the browser and paste it back.

Then leave it running. You'll see lines like "downloading…" and "npm ci…"; that's normal.

Finished when you see "Done." If it shows "still to fix" instead, do what each line says (or send the owner a screenshot), then paste the same line again. Running it again is always safe: it only fills in what's missing.

20–40 minutes the first time

3

Open the project in Claude

  1. In the Claude app, go to the Code tab.
  2. Choose the video-factory folder: C:\Users\you\video-factory on Windows, or /Users/you/video-factory on a Mac.
  3. When it asks whether to trust this folder, click Yes. This turns on automatic updates.
  4. Type: which styles can you make? It should list the styles.

The cloud factory and its GPUs are already connected; there's nothing else to sign in to.

2 minutes

You're set up. Now just ask

Always open the Claude app's Code tab in the video-factory folder. Talk to Claude normally:

  • "Make a gta video from this voiceover."Made on your computer
  • "Make an aviation video about this crash."Made on your computer
  • "Make a 2-minute flatglow video about volcanoes."Cloud GPU; Claude asks before it costs money
  • "From now on, paper-style titles are always in capitals."Saved for the whole team
  • Cloud videos arrive as a Google Drive link in the chat. Videos made on your computer are saved on your computer.
  • Updates are automatic. New styles, fixed tools and team rules reach you the next time you open Claude.
  • Your changes are shared too. If you change a style or a kit, everyone on the team gets it.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The window closed, or the computer restartedOpen it again and paste the same line. It continues where it stopped.
"still to fix" with a listDo what each line says, then paste the same line again. Stuck? Send the owner a screenshot.
"refused your access key" or "does not look right"Your key was replaced or removed, or the line got cut off when copying. Ask the owner for a fresh line and paste that one.
"Could not download the project"Check the internet connection, then paste the same line again.
Windows: "winget is not recognized"Install App Installer from the Microsoft Store, then paste the line again.
Anything elseIn the Claude app's Code tab, in the video-factory folder, type: "Setup isn't working. Run node scripts/vf.mjs setup and fix what it lists."
Keep your line to yourself: anyone using your key gets your access, and cloud videos cost the team money. Lost it or think someone saw it? Tell the owner; they can switch it off and send you a new one.

## Findings
1. Biggest cost is the pod's TTS: ~20 of the 24.5 min before previews. With an uploaded voiceover (drive_file_id) previews take ~3 min → a 30 min video ≈ **27 min end to end**.
2. A 192 vCPU CPU pod rendered at ~1.9x real time (better than the 1.59x measured on a 48 vCPU GPU pod): render is Chromium raster, CPU-bound.
3. `wait_job until:preview` failed: "D1 query result exceeded the maximum response size limit of 50 MB" (1197 preview rows). Needs a capped query. Reviewed via local QA frames instead.
4. GPU creates refused everywhere although the catalog showed LOW stock; RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell was missing from the rank (added, d7edb7c).
5. MCP connector timed out several times during long polls; D1 state polling worked as fallback.
6. QA flags were cosmetic (labels a few px into the top safe margin on keyframed items, a few overlaps); the simple auto-fix only handles static `at`.
